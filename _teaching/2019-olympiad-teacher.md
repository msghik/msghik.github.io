Teaching mathematics and computer science to talented students preparing for national and international olympiads.

Responsibilities
======
* Preparing gifted students for mathematics and computer science competitions
* Teaching advanced problem-solving techniques and algorithmic thinking
* Developing curriculum for competition preparation
* Mentoring students for national and international olympiads
* Conducting workshops and training sessions

Teaching Areas
======
* **Mathematics**: Number theory, combinatorics, algebra, geometry
* **Computer Science**: Algorithms, data structures, dynamic programming, graph theory
* **Problem Solving**: Competition strategies, time management, proof techniques

Competition Preparation
======
* Iranian National Computer Olympiad (INOI)
* Iranian National Mathematics Olympiad
* International Olympiad in Informatics (IOI) preparation
* Problem-solving workshops and mock competitions

Impact
======
* Successfully prepared multiple students for national competitions
* Developed comprehensive training materials and problem sets
* Created engaging learning environment for talented students
* Fostered interest in mathematics and computer science among youth

Skills Developed
======
* Advanced teaching techniques for gifted students
* Curriculum development for competitive mathematics and CS
* Mentoring and motivation strategies
* Competition preparation and coaching
